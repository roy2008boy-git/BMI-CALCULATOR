BMI Calculator

A simple BMI Calculator project that allows users to enter their height and weight, calculate their Body Mass Index (BMI), and view general food suggestions based on the result.

Features

- Enter height and weight
- Calculate BMI instantly
- Display the calculated BMI
- Show a general BMI category
- Provide simple food suggestions
- Simple and user-friendly interface

How It Works

1. Enter your height.
2. Enter your weight.
3. Click the Calculate BMI button.
4. The application calculates your BMI.
5. The result and general food suggestions are displayed.

BMI Formula

BMI is calculated using:

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m²)
